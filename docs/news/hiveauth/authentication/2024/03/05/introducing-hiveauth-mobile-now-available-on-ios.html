Introducing HiveAuth Mobile - Now available on iOS

Mar 5, 2024 • arcange

I'm happy to release the HiveAuth Mobile app for iOS, a user-friendly and secure app to store your keys on your iPhone

@arcange
![](https://i.imgur.com/Ldao03J.png)
A few months ago, I announced the [HiveAuth Mobile App for Android](/@arcange/introducing-hiveauth-mobile-android) After several postponements due to unforeseen circumstances, I am happy to finally be able to announce the official launch of the long-awaited HiveAuth Mobile for iOS. The HiveAuth Mobile is designed to make your Hive experience smoother, safer, and more intuitive. The app’s simplicity ensures a more approachable experience for newcomers. I invite you to read the [previous post](/@arcange/introducing-hiveauth-mobile-android) for more information about the app. I plan to write a user guide soon to cover in detail the app's features in-depth, although it is very easy to use. ### How to Get Started? #### Install and configure the app * Download the app from [Apple Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hiveauth/id6475395161). * Import your private keys. * Done!
PS: Android Users can download the app from [Google Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.hiveauth.mobile). ### Acknowledgement Once again, I would like to thank @sagarkothari88 for the help provided in porting the app to iOS. Creating an application for Apple's environment is like an obstacle course and his help and patience with an Apple noob like me was incredibly valuable. ### Support Support for the app is provided on Discord. I created a dedicated HiveAuth server where you can ask questions, write suggestions, report issues or get help as a developer. You can join our Discord by following this link: https://discord.gg/VxtYPatjPG ---
