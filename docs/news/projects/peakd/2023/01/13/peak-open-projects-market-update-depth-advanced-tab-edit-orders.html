Peak Open Projects - Market Update (Depth, Advanced Tab, Edit Orders)

Jan 13, 2023 • peak.open

New updates for market. Market depth, advanced tab, edit orders, and more

@peak.open
[Market Project](https://hivehub.dev/market) has recently pushed some updates on [HiveHub.Dev](https://hivehub.dev/market). Let us take a look on some of the recent changes! ![market.png](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23tRzKMGEPoE3PtP8kyzkY4sebj6xshfr5NvmkD54tbWb637tRrTGdNNpqaP3hia152sz.png) The code for all projects developed and maintained as part of [our proposal](https://peakd.com/proposals/223) is available here: https://gitlab.com/peakd *We do our best to open the repositories as soon as we consider the code to be stable enough and we have completed our initial brainstorm on the implementation.* Some direct links to repositories already available: * HiveHub.dev: https://gitlab.com/peakd/hivehub.dev * Hive Node Beacon: https://gitlab.com/peakd/hive-node-beacon * PeakD Hivemind Plugin: https://gitlab.com/peakd/peakd-hivemind-plugin * Others coming soon ![div.png](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/Eo1vTqKnBVeZz5H2tAZ8uu6um28SesjsB9d59L4bkpSr9UKyVRNYUdT5vRpCDrdGMtT.png) ### Market [HiveHub.dev module] Developers: @igormuba, @asgarth. Feedback and suggestions: @jarvie Repository: https://gitlab.com/peakd/hivehub.dev/-/tree/main/src/market #### Market Depth Chart ![Market Depth](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/EqFvLG5ZbXrHW9iSgyJhWTYvgR41RDUg8hmyjjkJDW5F5f54tT8yVeZjS4sXpGL4uTw.png) Market depth chart is now available on LIMIT and ADVANCED tab. This should make it much easier to view the volume on bids and ask side 😉 _Tip: Hover above the column to see the corresponding bid or ask at that price._ #### Advanced Tab Next, we have also made the Advanced tab available. This is a first attempt to provide a page with all trade tools visible at the same time. We are eager to get some feedback on how to make it even better or easier to use. ![Advanced tab](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23yJX3AwXsd5KgHGYA3PEg6y8FniprgCaa1HsTWNHTR4CmMpdC8e5Z1wMvNxJ8a3ZEUVB.png) Under Advanced, you can view the following in a single glance: * Price or Market Depth chart * Buy and Sell HIVE interface * Open Orders, including option to Edit or Delete them * Order Book * Trade History #### Editing Orders In our previous market update, we have shown that the LIMIT orders were already available on the website. At that time, open orders can only be cancelled -- which means that you'll have to cancel and place your order again when you have to change the values. But now, it's much easier! We have added the `Edit` action button for your open orders. ![Screenshot 2023-01-12 at 5.35.25 PM.png](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23tv8njnMRXr1xcSRDNuVjKkatfDPvMqj2poQV6AArJQo7oZ7GKxbtjgekHARM7Kf5CJH.png) To use this, simply hover to the My Orders portion, and then click Edit. It should show you a pop-up that is similar to the one below. ![Edit Buy Order pop-up](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23wBZq9zJ9oRvQQf9TCm6KeQ7ueoeRerSjLKt3479ao1zembbLwVn2tJFzMfmTcfvdYe3.png) Just simply update the values that you want to edit, and then click confirm. Aside from the ones highlighted, we have also pushed a couple more updates for the market platform. Here is the complete list of recent changes. **Recent activities:** ``` - 1 hour and 24 hours volume in trade history [COMPLETED] - Up to four decimal places in Last Trade Ratios [COMPLETED] - Improve limit page chart colors and header [COMPLETED] - Added Advanced page [COMPLETED] ``` --- ![Selection_298.png](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/peakd/w36DQpO7-Selection_298.png) ## How to support the @peakd project **WORK WITH US** If you're a project looking to integrate one of these tools message us.
