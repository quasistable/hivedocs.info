🐝 Automated Hive Status Badges - Bee themed HP Badges 🐝

Oct 24, 2024 • peak.open

New automated badges that give levels to how much HIVE power you hold

@peak.open
![image.png](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/peakd/EoKADo26HfxspHmHe3eWd5YrdrFjFUEJUvNRbPJtejrdGczM5hikwtADZYhDYsfX7hz.png) **Hive Badges** are badges created by the Hive community that can be implemented into any UI **Automated Badges** are built on open source software anyone can use and adapt for their own badges. ### **LEVELS FOR STAKED HIVE** The @peak.open team has created many automated badges and is now created a new class of automated badges based on Core Hive Blockchain stats. This time it's a badge to represent **Stake Hive holdings (HP)**. The @PeakD team has already implemented them into their website! Starting today, these badges will appear on users’ profiles, proudly showcasing their Staked Hive aka Hive Power (HP) We hope this starts a trend of automated badges that represent provable aspects of a person's account. #### ABOUT THE BEE BADGES Each badge is inspired by a unique type of bee (or it's cousin the wasp... aka social Hive/nest builders). Similar to what Hive Power does on Hive blogging sites this set of automated badges will reflect user influence and strength within the Hive ecosystem. Like Minnow to Whales? Similar to the common ocean creature level system used in other blockchains these badges offer a cool way for Hive to recognize and celebrate your contribution to the Hive blockchain. A naming level system that can be uniquely Hive's. Here’s a breakdown of the badge levels: ###### Worker Bee: 2.5k - 5k HP Diligent and hardworking, the Worker Bee is the backbone of the hive, always busy collecting and building. ![Worker Bee](https://images.hive.blog/400x400/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AJj1oHQ7P5DDQhPyvuZVReEKw6oAf83kTeCT7WBvaytoroozsJtDopmKfRxyXZD.png) ###### Honey Bee: 5k - 15k HP The Honey Bee is the most recognizable and symbolizes sweetness and productivity, tirelessly working to sustain the hive with its golden rewards. ![Honey Bee](https://images.hive.blog/400x400/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AKGchzKrnEqAfw1eHqkZQJt9uJciisQRhwXnpccXwFGdmEsGo5ot44vATx9hmgE.png) ###### Bumblebee: 15k - 50k HP The Bumblebee, larger and more resilient, thrives with purpose, its seemingly happy buzzing presence is impossible to ignore. ![Bumblebee](https://images.hive.blog/400x400/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AKRCj2aFPEP1q5Ls5j4jdA6pgt1SdYsUqvcA2D627Akok8zUejEALxrmg9CQm5J.png) ###### Yellowjacket: 50k - 200k HP Fast and fierce, the Yellowjacket is quick to defend the hive, bringing an edge of power and agility. ![Yellowjacket](https://images.hive.blog/400x400/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AKCJw1wHED3UaUMJ4sZbxBzUekqcRJBTHLrZFGG87uyrdPvoxDuejXJDFo2xJgL.png) ###### Giant Hornet: 200k - 1M HP Commanding and imposing, the Giant Hornet strikes fear with its size and strength. ![Giant Hornet](https://images.hive.blog/400x400/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AK6Ykrg9ThU8uWjkYzkjKk8ggd5kTo53QyoQboMokSCBo7RMamXQd5nhUSF2BsK.png) ###### Killer Bee: 1M - 5M HP A force to be reckoned with, the Killer Bee is both feared and admired for its relentless energy and power. ![Killer Bee](https://images.hive.blog/400x400/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AK57BL7NKYrzcvzHuRASSwpyHTxeWzSZQTjQDbKx7JtW6Ud19xpFGz7KnZTae5L.png) ###### Queen Bee: 5M+ HP Regal and unmatched, the Queen Bee is a status of achievement and greatness. ![Queen Bee](https://images.hive.blog/400x400/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AJj1u3Ex34Rezzjrgeab9Q58FvhfiFyjqkdw5ha8SjgES14CtL4GhYt5AcvMo72.png) We wanted the badge designs to reflect the energy and purpose of each bee type while keeping them fun, clean, and impactful. Every badge is inspired by the unique traits of its namesake, blending semi-realistic style with vibrant colors to stand out on your profile. -------- ### USE ON HIVE Each of these badges will be automatically connected to users. Since Hive badges are meant for decentralized usage every Hive app/website is able to easily connect them to users and display them. Since they are automated and based on Blockchain information they will just automatically update. Remember: The more HP you hold, the more powerful your badge becomes! Again it should be noted PeakD.com will be displaying them uniquely on user profiles right at the launch of the badges. ⭐️ A special thanks to @madhiyari, who implemented key data we needed from the [HafSQL](https://peakd.com/hive-139531/@mahdiyari/public-hafsql-database-on-haf) database, making this feature possible. Let us know what you think, will you be staking more on Hive to get to that next level?! 💪
#### ⏰ Coming Soon: Badge Assignments for Hive Engine Tokens We’re also finalizing a template that will allow badge assignments based on your Hive Engine tokens—whether they’re liquid or staked! This means even more ways to showcase your influence across the Hive ecosystem.
