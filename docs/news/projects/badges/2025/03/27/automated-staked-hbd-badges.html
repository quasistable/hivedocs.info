Automated Staked HBD BADGES

Mar 27, 2025 • peak.open

A new Hive Badge for those who've staked HBD

@peak.open
![image.png](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/peak.open/23wgYpF43Ve7ANxid3gEekEgaWmAMmFfF8KnBxAimdV9k5eyLaQA3BWz5MonioopHSDpL.png) 👑 **Automated Hive Savings Badges - Royalty-themed HBD Staking Badges 👑** We encourage other projects to use this open-source code to help them make other Hive Badges. **Introducing the HBD Savings Badges** We're happy to unveil a new set of automated badges, proudly crafted for general benefit to the Hive community and available to be integrated into your favorite Hive Project UI! Badges in general are for all of Hive and Built upon open-source software, these badges recognize and celebrate your commitment to the Hive ecosystem similar to the saved HIVE badge. **Royalty Has Arrived on Hive!** Inspired by the timeless elegance and prestige of royal hierarchies, our new badges celebrate the prudent savers and investors among us. With levels ranging from humble squires to powerful monarchs, these badges offer an engaging way to visually acknowledge your commitment to financial growth and stability within the Hive ecosystem. Badges are great ways to show accomplishment and staking HBD is an accomplishment. We believe these badges will inspire users to stake more, encourage responsible financial behavior, and foster greater recognition of users' dedication to the Hive blockchain. As these badges are automated and blockchain-based, your status updates in real-time—providing a dynamic, accurate reflection of your HBD holdings. ### Decentralized Integration These badges are automatically linked to your Hive account and built on open-source technology. This means any Hive-based app or website can integrate and display your badges seamlessly, and they’ll continuously update according to your actual HBD savings amount. They are available right now on PeakD.com and Hivehub.dev. Of note: there are tools on PeakD to change which badges you display and the order you display them on that site. ### Make your own badges Part of this open source project for badges is making the automated code to apply the badges and giving developers access to that code so they can easily make automated badges like this for their own projects. --- What automated badges would you like to see next?
