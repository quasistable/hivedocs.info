Introducing the Power Guardian Badges

Aug 14, 2025 • peak.open

A new badge to celebrate with those who have never powered down Hive

@peak.open
## A new way to recognize strength and commitment on Hive We’re excited to unveil a new badge series developed by the Peak Open team: **The Power Guardian Collection**, a tribute to those who stay powered up and help strengthen the Hive network. ![](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/asgarth/23tS2pRuMmpaErKs4pwEXR1GaaL2DPQWPPpYk1QzuLTvV48EitznSwwxiPH2MSussqD1c.png) These digital badges are automatically awarded to accounts **who have never powered down HIVE** based on their **staked Hive Power (HP)**. Each badge symbolizes a tier of commitment and resilience, styled in a unified **modern-fantasy visual theme**. While it may not be for everyone There is a certain segment of the Hive community that takes pride in having never powered down their Hive Power. This new badge series is designed to celebrate their commitment and resilience. We recognize that powering down is a personal choice and an important part of Hive’s flexibility, so this badge is simply a way to honor those who have chosen to stay powered up continuously without implying any judgment on those who power down from time to time. --- ## The 4 Power Guardians Badges | 🔹 *Initiate* *500 – 2,000 HP*
The beginning of power. Bold and glowing, this badge symbolizes your first step into deeper Hive engagement.
![](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AK51cpc1G25BzeHxir5xmTCNxiHKiYA5bP375cqia19kQKrNicz41R2YXjMLVpg.png) | 🔹 *Keeper* *2,000 – 5,000 HP*
A guardian of growing strength. Marked by control, responsibility, and a firm stance, the Keeper maintains and protects their stake.
![](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AJmnCKY6enH8dc15Xc87VgT1tpRmQJk1NYBPGeTGjRLH96kuQ1Q3Wq6bNRVo9T6.png) | | --- | --- | | 🔹 *Sentinel* *5,000 – 10,000 HP*
Always vigilant, the Sentinel watches over the network with strength and awareness. A symbol of steady power and presence.
![](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AJhU5opxUKRbUuJh2uqkCh5NTk4avACLigKkGJU4hxqCixh1EAJsGJEdVL1KLqe.png) | 🔹 *Warden* *10,000+ HP*
The ultimate defender. Ancient, unyielding, and steadfast, the Warden badge marks those with massive HP and unwavering loyalty to Hive.
![](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/yozen/AJg2psEzJqKXp8d1keX5njVhh2HFzvrszGhVeLTztChaw4Xzyw3kTMUarMtsNUK.png) | These badges are granted to **accounts with the listed HP levels who have not initiated a power down**. If you qualify, you should already have your badge! This is part of our ongoing work to celebrate contributions to Hive’s long-term strength and decentralization.
