Let's start Polling: Decentralized Polling

Apr 2, 2024 • peak.open

Create, vote, and engage with the polls UI on HiveHub

@peak.open
![Polls (1).png](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23w2HG4CxbUHM8TywfWaN3rNbPb6Ywboig9yzK7bEAnA5nGPbsmJeh9Riie5jCwfJyDTM.png) ### Polls now on HiveHub.dev It's time to start polling! https://hivehub.dev/polls and https://peakd.com/ both supporting with more sites online soon We previously announced the [Initial version of Open Polls Protocol](https://peakd.com/hive-139531/@peak.open/open-polls-protocol-initial-version) which aims to give the Hive community a way to vote (do a poll) on Hive that every single UI can easily integrate with, has an advanced analytics features, multiple voting qualifications, and filters. We're excited to announce that the Polls feature is now available through the HiveHub interface! *Many interfaces will be adding support for polls however at a minimum you can vote using https://hivehub.dev/polls **PeakD.com** has also finished their integration for seeing the results and for voting however to create a poll we still suggest hivehub.dev/polls* **WHAT TO KNOW ABOUT FIRST ITERATION OF POLLS** - 30 day polling limit on our interface to start with (Can be changed later) - There is an ability to filter by account age - Right now the only voting interpretation is Votes by Accounts. - Next feature will be staked HIVE voting - There will be other voting interpretation - We also hope to have other voting styles ... right now it's ONE VOTE multiple choice. - PeakD.com is ready to release ability to VOTE and to see RESULTS - Ecency is also working with the @peak.open team to release something soon as well - Open Polls uses the HAF technology developed by the @blocktrades team https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/peakd/Eq5HLJHhYHKBw2S16ZmhdSYfRE9jeHpAgyGmzZHX9PeBw22iaAKaKgjv2ipt4r7xy7Y.png ## How does it work? ### Creating a Poll **Creating a poll** is EASY. 1. Simply navigate to the Polls feature under Tools on HiveHub, 2. then click on the ``Create a New Poll`` button. 3. After that, the *Create a New Poll* window will pop-up, then you simply have to fill out the input fields with the corresponding information. It's that simple! ![Create a New Poll button](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/Eo8XcEkVsPQ2tsgDGW937ek5r1ANL7FWRpgGXoFF7q8oLUXShN8zS9hbrkP1Cnt3UdG.png) ![Create a New Poll input fields](https://images.hive.blog/750x750/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23tHV6AQfPBbfearUZafRUhtZUNBL2dE82q6yPtq87kZG6m4XzsCbWPruPJFox13kRx6h.png) You will also be given an option to ``Customize Hive post content``. From here, you have a couple of options to choose from to customize the poll to your own liking such as Thumbnail URL, Community, and Tags. ![Customize Hive post content](https://images.hive.blog/700x700/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23tHb6kphoRQ9ZMa26CCze3QKTvEn8BGuEPxH9Qcb23ytDjyNPVGxmbVGNCPRLrQB8zV1.png) ### Viewing other Polls Navigating through the Polls interface is quite straightforward. You can see all the polls created by the Hive community once you go to the [Polls UI on HiveHub](https://hivehub.dev/polls). ![Viewing Polls](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23t88adoN576KeL7AJpuKDLBAhp3b26BJ5WqQ9255jzMnGZKDWbuwMHXFeE1dyRau7gXR.png) From there, you will also have an option to sort the list by clicking on the column headers (e.g. by Creation Date, End Date, Total Votes). **FILTERS** If you want to see the polls based on specific criteria such as Post Title, Poll Question, Post Author, and Author, you can use the Filter functionality as shown in the example below. ![Filtering Polls](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23ynUgR5xH7zK6saCsnWW1oeKhYsj2JudGamcnYbCAtvrWSCJFCMUif9YagXeWGVPzBzB.png) This should help you easily see polls that are more relevant to you, especially when there are already a lot of polls created by the Hive community! **VIEWING SPECIFIC POLLS** *Want to see more details about a specific poll?* Of course you can! If you want to see more details about a specific poll, you simply have to click on the *Question* for that poll, and then a new window will load containing more information such as the current votes, tags, and additional information. ![Click on the Poll Question](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23tHZbtPEqyM8xFtMPVGvz5fXc7sE88MvuvZZbFWYeYFKiKa51Ehi4VMngYeTeq2SML8X.png) The example below is for a test poll about phone brands used. As you can see, an analytics of the voting and results is shown upon clicking of the poll question, as well as the tags and additional information. ![Phone brand sample poll](https://images.hive.blog/750x750/https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/mozzie5/23tSwyV7zaVSHRi2EwqGwPTv8DaQHsZDaMckaZATZPC2y3GmjQEnaPd5SAtXUSokrayQB.png) ### Time to start voting! Whether it is to create a poll for your own community or for the entire Hive community, the choice is up to you! (Or you can also make a poll to make that choice 😉). This is the time to have fun with this new tool for improved engagement, decision making, getting feedback that the Peak Open Projects team has created for the Hive community. Head over to [Polls on HiveHub](https://hivehub.dev/polls) and start creating your first poll today! Don't forget to share your thoughts, ideas, and, of course, your votes with the rest of the community. We are looking forward to see the creative polls you come up with. *Got questions or feedback about the new polls feature? Drop them in the comments below or reach out to us directly. Your input is invaluable as we continue to improve and enhance the experience of the Hive community.* --- ![Selection_298.png](https://files.peakd.com/file/peakd-hive/peakd/w36DQpO7-Selection_298.png) #### *The Peak Open Projects Team* [Discord](https://discord.gg/Fy3bca5_) - [Blog](https://peakd.com/@peakd) - [Twitter](https://twitter.com/peakdcom) - [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/peakd/) ## **SUPPORT US** Help us build the future of Hive-based networking.
