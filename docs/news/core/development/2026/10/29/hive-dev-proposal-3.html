Hive Dev Proposal #3

Oct 29, 2026 • hdev

Hdev team DHF proposal 3 for development focused on growing hive core code, apps and projects serving hive and bringing it further to the world

@hdev
Since mid-2024, our team has been building Hive tools in the HAF ecosystem, working alongside @blocktrades and the core development team. This is our third Hive Dev proposal, and we want to start with a big thank you to everyone who supported the first two. ![Hive logo above the words "Dev Proposal #3", with three developers at a laptop, an analytics dashboard and mobile apps](https://usermedia.actifit.io/MVU1N298R4N5J3DDRT8S3NYZDYVWZD) Proposal #2 was funded for only about the second half of its term. During that period, 25 people contributed through our work, and we merged **434 merge requests** across 10 Hive repositories and shipped: - **Two new HAF apps:** **haf\_stats** (network analytics) and **haf\_fyp** (a personalised For You feed), both now standard modules in the latest haf\_api\_node release. **PeakD serves haf\_fyp's ranking** to its readers. - **hivescan.info as an analytics hub:** a dashboard you arrange yourself, sign-in with Keychain and HiveSigner, account analytics with a delegation map, a **DHF Governance Hub**, and network, RC and governance reports in 12 languages. - **New endpoints and fixes in Hive's core backends:** Balance Tracker, HAF Block Explorer, Hivemind, HiveSense and HAF, including rebuilt escrow tracking, corrected witness vote counts, and a Hivemind bug that silently skipped blocks, which we found and diagnosed on our node. - **Two public HAF API nodes** that we run, carrying the full stack. - **Apps that bring people to Hive:** Waggle; HivePulse, now a Hive wallet with a Scam Shield, on Chrome and Firefox; Hive Runner; four apps, not yet launched, from our **summer developer cohort**; Actifit's on-chain challenges engine, **The Arena**, with redesigned Android and iOS apps; weekly contests that pay Hive writers in HIVE, run in our new Hdev Contests community; and **Vylor**, a finance app built this year and live on Google Play and the App Store. Our ask is 275 HBD/day, about 34% less than our prior proposal's adjusted rate, after streamlining our developer allocation and infrastructure. Below is what we delivered, what comes next and what the ask pays for. Thank you for reading, and for helping us keep improving Hive. ## Summary In the year of proposal #2 (2025-11-01 to 2026-10-28) the team merged **434 merge requests across 10 Hive GitLab repositories**. It is the data layer behind hivescan.info's analytics and reports. Because it ships in the standard HAF API node stack, any node operator can serve it and any front end can build on it. [Repository](https://gitlab.syncad.com/hive/haf_stats). - **Network statistics:** daily active users, vote stats, content volume, engagement, a top-accounts leaderboard, HP distribution and author retention and signup-cohort funnels. Values are checked against a real 5M-block replay. ([daily active users](https://testapi.hivescan.info/haf-stats-api/network/daily-active-users), [top accounts](https://testapi.hivescan.info/haf-stats-api/network/top-accounts)) - **Checked against Hivemind:** post counts match Hivemind exactly on all 3,864 days from 2016-03-24 to 2026-10-21, and comment counts stay within 2%. That gap came from a Hivemind sync issue we reported and the core team fixed on October 26, [hivemind#394](https://gitlab.syncad.com/hive/hivemind/-/issues/394). Two counting bugs found on the way, a double count and a ~19% over-report, were fixed first. ([the check](https://gitlab.syncad.com/hive/haf_stats/-/merge_requests/124)) - **Governance:** stake concentration (Gini coefficient, top-1% and top-10% shares) and per-witness missed-block stats. ([influence concentration](https://testapi.hivescan.info/haf-stats-api/governance/influence-concentration)) - **Resource Credits:** network RC utilization, per-account RC footprint and a DApp footprint that maps custom\_json to named apps in 9 categories. It adds a per-event RC timeline for each account, and RC estimates calibrated against the node's own rc\_api (the old model priced a typical custom\_json about 3× too high). Since October, RC uses each transaction's **recorded cost** where the node has it. ([RC footprint](https://testapi.hivescan.info/haf-stats-api/account/actifit/rc-footprint)) - **Communities and social graph:** an account's activity per community (each reply traced up to its root post, checked against Hivemind on 120 real threads), its top partners for replies, votes, transfers, reblogs and follows, and a network-wide DApp usage leaderboard by app or category. These power hivescan.info's community, Sankey and DApp-usage reports. - **Account finances:** a per-account financial summary covering 15 operation types, incoming and outgoing. - **Performance:** a year of daily active users went from 7.8 s to 4.6 ms (~1,700× faster), a heavy account's RC footprint from ~104 s to ~4.3 s and the 365-day top-accounts query from ~22 s to 0.98 s. A nightly refresh's slowest read fell from 8.3 s to 2–4 ms, and the network DApp usage leaderboard from ~20 s to about 0.55 s. A full rebuild of account financial history went from tens of hours to 1–2 hours. All were measured on our node, testapi. - **Operations:** a safe install, upgrade and uninstall, tested in CI against the real installer; a version endpoint and a corrected sync status; a published image; and a published disk footprint for operators (about 125 GB on compressed ZFS). It also fixed a stall in which haf\_stats, waiting on Hivemind, could hold up Hivemind and its API on the whole node. It ships as a standard module in the latest release, 1.28.8, of the [haf\_api\_node](https://gitlab.syncad.com/hive/haf_api_node) stack. ### haf\_fyp — personalised For You feed (new HAF app) The open-source discovery engine promised as item 9 of proposal #2: a personalised For You feed computed from HAF data. Front ends get a ready-made feed in Hive bridge format, readers get posts picked for them, and a score explainer shows why each post was picked. [Repository](https://gitlab.syncad.com/hive/haf_fyp). - **Ranking:** recency, engagement, author credibility (stake and reputation) and semantic relevance from HiveSense embeddings, with a per-community diversity cap. New readers pick a few interests, so their first feed is never empty. Posts the reader or their community muted are left out. - **Ranking that works as designed:** only 0.31% of posts had a community recorded, so the community boost barely applied; it is now 21.35%, with a stronger boost for communities a reader subscribes to. New readers' interest profiles were never built for established communities, and readers who picked interests but never voted were never re-ranked, so their feeds sat 12–15 days old. All three are fixed. - **Fresh for people who read it:** a reader's feed used to be about 11 hours old on average, and up to 22. Readers who open their feed are now ranked first, and a read that arrived during a ranking pass no longer waits 12–16 minutes but about 2 s; in a test on our node, a stale feed was re-ranked 9 s after it was opened. ([MR](https://gitlab.syncad.com/hive/haf_fyp/-/merge_requests/99)) - **API:** personalised and global feeds in Hive bridge format, a score explainer and an algorithm registry. A 20-post page is served in about 0.13–0.16 s end to end. Rendered posts are cached, so on the server a 20-post page takes 35 ms personalised (was 911 ms) and 12 ms global (was 345 ms). ([global feed](https://testapi.hivescan.info/haf-fyp-api/v1/fyp/global), [a personal feed](https://testapi.hivescan.info/haf-fyp-api/v1/fyp/feed/mcfarhat)) - **Telemetry:** front ends report impressions, opens and dwell time, and the app rolls them into daily metrics. It can also A/B test two ranking versions against each other. PeakD has sent these events since October 6: 322 from 18 accounts by October 15. - **Security:** changes to a reader's interests must be signed with their posting key, and delegated posting authority is accepted. Readers can opt out with a signed request; the same change fixed a bug that made saving interests fail for every reader on some installs. Rate limits apply per client, charged before a request is read when the node gateway identifies the caller. An opt-in setting stops forged reads from claiming ranking slots. - **Adoption:** **PeakD** serves haf\_fyp's ranking to its readers. From September 23 to October 15, 326 of 442 feed requests (74%) came from PeakD, for 105 accounts, on all 23 days. We measure this from request origins in our node's logs. Our own Waggle and HivePulse's For You tab run on it too, and Denser plans to adopt it. - **Operations:** one query had used about 98% of the app's CPU and starved the whole node: Block Explorer and Balance Tracker fell 43 blocks behind and returned errors. A partial index fixed it (load from ~5.7 to ~1.6) and both recovered. Node operators can now tune feed freshness: on our node a full re-ranking pass went from ~22 h to under 4 h. It also gained a /metrics health endpoint, a personalised fallback when HiveSense is down, HAF app locks, truthful health checks, install lanes tested against real HAF in CI and a published image. It ships as a standard module in the latest haf\_api\_node release, 1.28.8. ![Waggle screenshot](https://usermedia.actifit.io/MVU1H2L3LTJ01GN2UMRCJ33JHQTVK) *Waggle, running on haf\_fyp, shows each reader why a post was picked: relevance, recency, engagement and credibility, plus a boost for communities they follow.* ### Block Explorer UI — hivescan.info The explorer front end. Everything in it serves one goal: bringing new users and new functionality to Hive. The plans by area follow. ### Existing Hive projects - **Block Explorer UI ([hivescan.info](https://hivescan.info)):** grow the analytics and reports built on haf\_stats, adding dashboards as new metrics land. Extend RC as recorded cost, already shown on the account and network RC screens, to every report. Widen what signed-in users can do, building on Keychain and HiveSigner sign-in and synced workspaces. Keep the explorer current with each HAF release. - **Backend HAF apps** (Balance Tracker, HAF Block Explorer, HiveSense): new endpoints and speed-ups as the explorer and other front ends need them. Sharper HiveSense semantic search, with author and time-range filters, no deleted posts in results and safer sync checks. The author filter is already in review (hivesense !149): a search for one large author used to return nothing after 12–14 s, and for 99% of authors a search drops from ~0.4–0.75 s to ~10–25 ms. Fixes upstream where we find problems. This year, for example, we reported that any app's role could empty the HAF tables that record every app's progress (haf #348), and the core team has since closed that hole in HAF. - **Public nodes:** keep testapi.hivescan.info and hiveapi.actifit.io current with each HAF release. testapi is where our new work goes live first. ### Our HAF apps: haf\_stats and haf\_fyp - **haf\_stats:** use each transaction's recorded RC across every report, and add network and account metrics as the explorer's reports call for them. Keep its queries fast as history grows, and keep it current in each haf\_api\_node release. - **haf\_fyp:** improve the ranking with the reading signals front ends now send: impressions, opens and dwell time. Expose the feed-freshness settings operators can now tune in the haf\_api\_node configuration, and make sure front ends' telemetry passes the node gateway. Bring the feed to more front ends, Denser included, and keep it current in the standard release. ### New: HAF AI HAF AI is an MCP server that gives AI agents (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others) citable, fresh access to Hive data that HAF nodes already serve. That covers HiveSense search, Hivemind posts and communities, HAfAH account history, haf\_stats metrics and haf\_fyp's ranked feed. Every answer links back to Hive, which turns AI answers into a way into Hive. It is read-only, holds no keys and runs no language model of its own. 1. **Pilot:** 7 tools on testapi.hivescan.info, with one real user through a Claude connector (about 3 weeks). 2. **Public listing:** account and community tools, metrics, the ranked feed, a haf\_api\_node module and a citation resolver (about 8 more weeks). This phase needs reviews from HiveSense and haf\_api\_node maintainers, one front end, and a second node operator. 3. **After listing:** topic briefs, onboarding answers and an agent-disclosure standard. 4. **Intelligence:** a curation score that resists gaming, profiles and topic tools, built with the HiveSense maintainers. Success target, 90 days after listing: 500 citation click-throughs a week, and 40 distinct callers a week with 15 of them returning. If it falls short (fewer than 5 returning callers, or fewer than 100 click-throughs a week), we reassess before building further. ### Other Hive projects on GitHub - **Waggle:** keep it as the reference front end for haf\_fyp, picking up new ranking features as they land and extending its "why this post" explanations. - **HivePulse:** keep improving the extension, its wallet, Scam Shield, For You tab and SEO analyzer, and keep running its weekly contests. - **Hive Runner:** take the game from public preview to launch, with its live block feed and on-chain scores. - **The summer cohort's apps** (Hive Freelance, LINGO, HiveLore, Hive Chameleon): take them from development to a public launch on Hive. ### Actifit - **The Arena:** more challenge types and contests on the on-chain challenges engine launched this year, building on its AFIT prizes and badges, and release it in the Android app. - **Apps and site:** keep improving the Android and iOS apps, and grow actifit.io alongside them. ### Vylor - Deepen its Hive integration. Planned items include HBD savings and transfers by voice, an on-chain backup and habit rewards. ## Proposal ask We are requesting **275 HBD/day** from 2026-12-01 to 2027-12-31, **about 34% less** than the adjusted rate of our recent proposal, [#360](https://peakd.com/proposals/360).
